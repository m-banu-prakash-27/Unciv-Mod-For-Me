# Magalanadu v4.9 Native Spy Operations

Enable **Espionage** in the game setup and play an offline game as Magalanadu. Open the normal **Espionage** screen. If you have no spies yet, select any Magalanadu unit and choose **Strategic Directorate → Espionage** to open that same screen.

1. Press **Recruit spy**. This creates a regular Unciv spy at the hideout. Magalanadu can recruit for free with no roster cap, one per press. Recruitment is available from the espionage screen.
2. Use the native **Move** action and wait for the spy to arrive and establish their network. For most operations, post the spy in the foreign city you want to target. Country-level operations use the city owner's civilization. Pair operations let you choose the second civilization.
3. Press **Operations** on that spy's row. Choose a mission and use Standard or Careful planning. The cost, timing, success estimate and exposure estimate are shown before launch.
4. The spy's action column shows the mission and its countdown. **Manage operation** lets you delay or abort it. **Spy reports** keeps a record of completed, failed and cancelled missions.

For a **Counterintelligence sweep** or **Expel a hostile spy**, post your spy in the Magalanadu city you want to defend and pick a rival. The sweep looks in that city. **Interrogate captured agent** and **Turn a captured agent** also run from a domestic posting.

Each stationed spy can run one operation at a time. The prior three-mission limit is lifted. A spy's effective rank improves mission odds. A queued operation temporarily pauses that spy's automatic vanilla activity. After completion or cancellation, their native activity resumes if they are still stationed. Reassignment, death, displacement or loss of the posting cancels the operation. Gold is spent at launch. Native movement restrictions, city occupancy, passive technology theft and City-State elections are part of the same system.

All 34 mission effects are listed in [the v4.8 mission catalogue](V4_8_INTELLIGENCE_AGENCY_GUIDE.md). This guide replaces that release's launch and scheduling instructions. Existing v4.8 networks and reports are preserved by the save schema. Unassigned v4.8 orders are refunded on the next eligible turn and can then be relaunched with a stationed spy.

## Build and test

Use the [v4.9.1 Android and Windows build guide](V4_9_1_UNCIV_UPDATE.md) for the current Unciv release.

Check recruitment with an empty roster. Recruit multiple spies, move one to a foreign city and wait for it to be ready. Launch an operation, save and reload, and confirm the assigned spy and countdown persist. Move a second spy during a mission to check cancellation. Use another civilization to confirm the extra controls are hidden.
