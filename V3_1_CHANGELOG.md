# v3.1 — Unit Export Contracts and City-State Gifting

- Retains all v3.0 data objects, art, upgrades, and active systems.
- Adds native City-State donation permissions to twenty Magalanadu foreign aid, science, military-aid and blueprint units.
- Makes each of the 52 costed custom units eligible for a scaled one-time Gold export grant on qualifying unit loss in non-hostile foreign owned territory.
- Improves City-State diplomacy through 25–110 Influence when gifting specific aid units, linked to unit cost.
- Adds +50% Gold from allied City-State actual production and +100% of City-State-supplied resource benefits.
- Does **not** debit recipient Gold or resources. Direct GPT/resource payments from major civilizations remain trade negotiations.
- Requires in-game test; engine lacks gift-specific economy triggers.
